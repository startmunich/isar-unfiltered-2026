**Tone rules — keep these pinned while writing or expanding anything below:**
Direct, human, confident, slightly provocative, honest, playful when it fits, unpolished in a good way, European, anti-bullshit. Short sentences. Say what you mean.

**Use:** build, ship, do, make it happen, show us what you built, unfiltered, real, proof, doers.
**Avoid:** visionary, ecosystem, disruptive, thought leaders, unlock your potential, entrepreneurial excellence, empowering, innovative minds, change-makers, game-changing.
